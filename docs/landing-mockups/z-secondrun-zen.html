Fast Checkout
BATCH S20 · FINTECH $124.0M Burned

“The fastest one-click checkout experience for the open internet.”

Burned $10M per month sustaining 400 employees and splashy sports sponsorships while generating under $600k in annual revenue. Shop Pay already satisfied the merchant need for free.

The 2026 Counter-Strategy: Headless, open-source 1-click checkout SDK for WooCommerce and custom React storefronts with 0% payment processing markup.

Pebble Technology
BATCH W11 · HARDWARE $43.0M Burned

“Customizable e-paper smartwatch with a 7-day battery life.”

Abandoned its cult minimalist niche to fight Apple Watch directly on color screens and voice apps. Overextended on physical inventory and venture debt before demand materialized.

The 2026 Counter-Strategy: Open-source, hackable e-paper productivity wearable with 14-day battery, zero subscription locks, and open HTTP notification webhooks.

ScaleFactor
BATCH W17 · FINTECH $104.0M Burned

“Automated AI bookkeeping and financial intelligence for small businesses.”

Promised artificial intelligence but secretly routed bookkeeping to dozens of human accountants in Austin and the Philippines. Books were riddled with errors and clients fled en masse.

The 2026 Counter-Strategy: Autonomous deterministic Plaid webhook reconciliation engine syncing double-entry ledgers in real-time for $79/mo flat with zero human CPAs.

Quibi
VENTURE ARCHIVE · MEDIA $1.75B Burned

“Quick bites of premium Hollywood storytelling designed exclusively for mobile screens.”

Spent $100,000 per minute on celebrity productions while forbidding social sharing and screenshotting. TikTok and YouTube provided superior vertical entertainment at zero content cost.

The 2026 Counter-Strategy: Decentralized, short-form micro-documentary knowledge base curated directly from verified academic and technical whitepapers.

Juicero
VENTURE ARCHIVE · IOT $120.0M Burned

“The first connected cold-press juice system with farm-fresh proprietary pouches.”

Built a $700 Wi-Fi connected aluminum press with custom gearboxes until Bloomberg demonstrated that the pouches could be squeezed more quickly with two human hands.

The 2026 Counter-Strategy: Direct-from-orchard freeze-dried organic cold-press powders delivered in biodegradable sachets requiring zero proprietary hardware.
