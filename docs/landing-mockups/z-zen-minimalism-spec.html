Design Philosophy · Zen Minimalism

Deliberate restraint where whitespace and typography are the design.

Nothing is added for decoration. Every element exists in quiet dialogue with empty space. Hierarchy arises not from heavy weight or strident accents, but from subtle variations in scale, position, and tonal depth.

05 · Non-Negotiable Canon

Six Design Constraints

01

Single-Column Discipline

All elements progress in a serene, unbranching vertical continuum. Multi-column grid systems and fragmented dashboards are entirely forbidden.

02

Weight 300 Monasticism

Bold weights are forbidden. True typographic hierarchy is expressed exclusively through spatial distance, point-size scale, and tonal differentiation.

03

Strict Trinary Palette

Maximum three tones: near-black (#1a1a18), weathered mid-gray (#999999), and off-white paper (#faf9f7). Zero accent colors, saturated links, or colored status badges.

04

Hairline Geometry

Borders shall never exceed 1 pixel in width. Shadows are restricted to imperceptible elevations below rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02). Heavy card surfaces are eliminated.

05

Contemplative Pace

Page entrances emerge over a tranquil 2-second ease-in. Rapid transitions, spring easings, parallax triggers, and frantic micro-interactions are rejected.

06

Remove What You Can

Perfection is achieved not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away. Every icon, illustration, or flourish is stripped.
